## What is PPIE?

PPIE means doing research *with* people, not just *about* them. Our public partners are not study participants. They are advisers who bring their own lived experience to the research.

## What our public partners do

- **Shape the questions:** tell us what matters most to families.
- **Review materials:** check that our information is clear and easy to read.
- **Share the results:** help us explain what we find to the public.
